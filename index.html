I have never run before. Can I pass SSC CPO 1600m in 6.5 minutes?

Yes. 70% of candidates who join us start with an 8+ minute 1600m run time. With our step-by-step stride cadence training and progressive stamina builds, cadets consistently clock below 6 minutes.

Do you provide training for female-specific physical standards?

Yes, our regular batches cover female-specific physical standards including the 800m run, female high jump requirements, and shot put technique, alongside the general training schedule.

What happens if I suffer from shin splints or heel pain?

We provide running gait analysis, running shoe guidance, turf recovery sessions, and specialized calf-strengthening protocols to keep you uninjured.
